# Venue estimate clarity — October 14, 2026

Some buylist descriptions presented illustrative retail ratios as instant
offers or typical rates. Card Kingdom, CoolStuffInc, Star City Games and
TCG Bulk now describe those ratios as assumptions and direct sellers to obtain
the buyer's actual quote before shipping. Unsupported "best rates" and
"biggest buylist" claims have been removed from those descriptions.
The shared workflow no longer promises an instant offer, fee breakdowns label
the ratio as an assumption, and payout timing directs sellers to confirm with
the buyer instead of presenting a generic 7–14 day range as a venue promise.

Cardmarket's region warning incorrectly said that international shipping and
conversion were excluded. The calculator already deducted entered international
postage and a modeled 3% conversion allowance. The warning, fee label and
accuracy page now explain that behavior. They also identify the $110 commission
cap as an approximation of €100, without live exchange-rate data. The public
currency help page lists EUR and GBP account currencies; none of this establishes
a USD withdrawal route.

The arithmetic, buylist ratios, ranking exclusions and Cardmarket account/postage
requirements are unchanged. This is a disclosure correction, not a fresh audit
of the complete venue terms. Cardmarket and CoolStuffInc retain September 1 fee
verification dates. Partial indexed policy text cannot justify a new date when
the complete policy could not be retrieved.
