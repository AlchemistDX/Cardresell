# Seller pilot protocol — prepared before recruitment

Status: prepared, not conducted. No participants have been contacted and no results are claimed. These are proposed internal acceptance thresholds, not advertised performance promises.

## Sample and recording

Recruit 5–10 independent sellers only after the owner authorizes outreach. Include at least two primarily Pokémon sellers and at least one each for Magic and Yu-Gi-Oh!, with both occasional and high-volume sellers. Record an anonymous participant ID, device/browser, experience, game and application commit. Do not record payment details, credentials, buyer addresses or full account exports.

Each seller uses five of their own cards with independently checked name, set, collector number and finish. Keep these images held out from scanner tuning until the run finishes. Include one low-value common, one older printing, one current printing, one promo or foil variant and one difficult real phone photo per seller. Record glare, blur, tilt, crop and slab status rather than assuming every difficult image is equivalent. Use both front and back where the workflow calls for them. Do not synthesize successful results or count repeated photos as additional independent cards.

## Tasks

1. Start signed out, find the free limits and explain the difference between identification and estimated grading.
2. Identify or search each card. Record automatic correct identity, review, wrong automatic identity, unsupported or unavailable. Record finish separately. Time from submitting the prepared photo to actionable results; include failed attempts and retries.
3. Explain the headline price, reported low/high, source/date, condition and whether it measures asks, a reference or sold evidence. Missing prices are a separate outcome from identification failure.
4. Compare payouts for a $1 card, a $10 card and a lot of 100 low-value cards. Change shipping once. Explain selling payout versus profit with unknown and then known acquisition cost.
5. Save the intended physical copies, set cost/location, prepare a draft, download the CSV and find the upload instructions. Test Seller Hub import only in the owner's authorized account; stop before publishing a live listing. Record accepted/rejected rows and the importer messages.
6. Reload and use a second device to confirm copies, cost, condition and location survive. Record a clearly designated test sale, inspect profit and export a backup. Do not alter real customer stock without specific authorization.
7. Repeat a normal visit after seven days and record whether the seller returns voluntarily, the task completed and reasons to pay or not pay. Do not initiate a purchase solely for this pilot.

## Thresholds set before running

- No wrong-card automatic selection, lost physical copy/cost, duplicate listing, duplicate credit charge/grant or unintended publication in the pilot. Any such observation blocks broad advertising until corrected and retested.
- At least 80% of participants complete scan/search → comparison → draft export without intervention. Report the numerator and denominator; with 5–10 participants, do not present this as a population success rate.
- At least 80% correctly explain reference/asking/sold evidence and payout versus profit without coaching.
- Report median and p95 end-to-end identification time, time to first comparison and time to export; do not silently discard failures or set a favorable timing threshold after seeing the data.
- Publish automatic/review/wrong/unsupported counts by game, plus unavailable-price and finish-uncertain counts. The supplied development photos remain a separate regression sample.
- Report seven-day returns and reasons to pay descriptively. This small pilot cannot establish retention or market demand statistically.

## Separate release gates

Actual Seller Hub acceptance, physical iPhone Safari camera/rotation/background/poor-network checks and current provider invoice reconciliation remain separate evidence. Passing browser fixtures does not close them. Record build/date/device and attach the actual result for each gate. Do not claim individual sold comps, automatic sales sync or universal game support unless separately implemented and verified.
