# Advertising entry workflow: respect visitor intent

October 13, 2026

The automatic first-visit example could reset a visitor's chosen game or select a card after they left Lookup or opened a form. The example now yields before starting and while waiting for catalogue results when the visitor has chosen another game, view, dialog, card or search. Cancelling the example withdraws only its own pending request; it cannot invalidate a newer visitor search. Idle visitors retain the existing automatic example.
