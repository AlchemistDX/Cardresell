Frequently asked questions

Is there really a free forever tier?

Yes. Verify your email and you get 10 ID scans + 1 AI Grade to start, then 5 ID scans + 1 AI Grade every month, forever. No card required, no trial expiry. If you sell a few cards a month, that may be all you need. Free-tier venue ranking shows eBay and TCGplayer.

What happens if I run out of credits?

Card price lookup keeps working — that's unmetered on every tier. Only the ID scan and AI Grade features pause until your next monthly refill or until you buy a one-time top-up pack. Nothing gets deleted, and your unused credits from previous months carry forward.

Do credits expire?

No. Monthly credits from Free or paid plans and one-time credit packs all carry forward indefinitely. If you cancel a paid plan, any credits you still have stay with you.

What's the refund policy?

Failed-scan refunds are the only refunds we issue. If our scanner can't confidently identify a card, the credit refunds automatically before you see a result. Wrong result? Tap “Not my card — refund credit” on the result screen and the credit is restored immediately — no email required.

Subscriptions can be cancelled anytime from inside the app and run through the end of the paid period. We do not prorate or refund partial periods. Credit packs are non-refundable once purchased. Credits never expire, so anything you buy stays on your account. Full policy in our Terms.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel from the pricing modal (opens from the header "Upgrade" button) or by emailing will@cardresell.org. Your paid access continues until the end of the billing period you've already paid for. No cancellation fee, no phone call, no dark patterns.

How is this different from PriceCharting, TCGplayer, or the PSA app?

Those tell you what a card sold for. We tell you where you'll get paid the most after each venue's fees, shipping, and cash-out rules. Same card, side-by-side net-payout comparison across 15 venues. See our Accuracy page for the exact fee schedule we use for every venue and how often we re-verify it.

How accurate is AI Grade?

It's a screening tool, not a professional grade. Use it to decide whether it's worth paying $30+ to submit a card to PSA/BGS/CGC, not to price finished inventory. We don't have a published validation set yet (planned: 100-card gold-set audit). Our Accuracy page covers our known limits honestly.

Are the affiliate links why you rank venues the way you do?

No. Ranking is by net payout, full stop — the same math runs whether or not we have an affiliate relationship with the winning venue. eBay and TCGplayer are affiliate partners, most others are not. When it matters, TCGplayer is often the highest-net-payout venue for singles, and our affiliate cut on TCGplayer is smaller than on eBay. If we were gaming ranking for commission, we'd push eBay harder.

Do you take my cards or handle money?

No. CardResell is a comparison tool. We don't buy your cards, we don't hold your funds, and we don't handle shipping. You keep your cards, you list on whichever venue we recommend (or any other), and the buyer pays that venue directly. Payments to CardResell for a paid plan go through Stripe.

What data do you send to OpenAI when I scan a card?

Just the image you upload, encrypted in transit, only for the duration of the identification/grading request. OpenAI doesn't train on API-mode traffic. We don't retain the image after the request completes. Full details in our Privacy Policy.
