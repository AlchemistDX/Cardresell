From finding a card to preparing a sale

Every plan includes access to card identification, Quick and Deep Grade estimates, camera or photo uploads, and listing-draft creation. Scans and grading use credits; drafting remains subject to account limits. Scanner and grader accuracy is the same across tiers.

Casual adds selling venues such as Mercari, Whatnot, Cardmarket and Cardsphere. Pro and Business also include supported buylist and consignment comparisons. Availability varies by card game; payouts are estimates, not guaranteed sale prices.

Every plan can save up to 500 private AI grade reports and up to 500 listing drafts. Save reports explicitly after grading and reopen them from Collection → Saved AI grades. Reports include results and notes, not photos. Individual and selected bulk-scan drafts share the same account limit and cost no additional scan credits.

Listing tools prepare your marketplace handoff; you publish through the marketplace yourself. Tier-specific monthly listing quotas and cloud photo allowances are not active.

All paid plans unlock tracking beyond 10 flips and Grading Log export. Every plan includes private saved AI grade reports.
