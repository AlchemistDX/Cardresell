### Shipping and Delivery Fees on Mercari

Mercari prepaid labels offer low, fixed rates with USPS, FedEx, and UPS for packages weighing up to 100 lbs. These
rates offer an average of 54% savings compared to the retail pricing of each carrier; all fees and rates are based
on a comparison completed January 6, 2026*.

The package must weigh less than or equal to the weight specified on the label and must be under 100 pounds or the longest side is not greater than 30” and the girth is not greater than 130”.
Shipping carriers will use either the package weight or the dimensional weight of the package. We do not support
national shipping for motorized items, furniture, bicycles or major appliances.

If your package weighs more than 100 pounds (either by weight or size), you’ll need to ship it on your own. You will
be responsible for all costs associated with the shipping process. Packages over 150 lb will be charged a flat freight rate of $2500.

If a return is approved for an item over 100 pounds or a package that exceeds the size limit, sellers are
responsible for providing a shipping label to receive the item back. Mercari’s recommendations for prepaid labels
when listing an item do not factor in the dimensional weight. If the app recommends a shipping label of 100 pounds
but the package for an approved return exceeds the weight or size limit, sellers will still be responsible for
providing a return label.

#### Mercari prepaid label rate updates:

Starting May 7, 2026, USPS Ground Advantage and Priority Mail prepaid label rates reflect USPS’s fuel surcharge. USPS First-Class Envelopes and Media Mail are not impacted. Read more in Shipping change announcements.

Mercari prepaid labels still offer an average of 54% savings* compared to retail carrier rates. Plus, every label
includes up to $200 in Shipping Protection and $20 for First-Class Envelope.

*Fees and rates are based on a comparison to the retail prices and surcharges for standard and economy shipments
of our carriers (USPS, UPS, FedEx) for items between 0.25 lb - 100 lbs. Comparison completed January 6, 2026.
