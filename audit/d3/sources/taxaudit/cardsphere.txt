You can maintain a Stored Value Balance with Cardsphere for the purpose of participation in the marketplace. Cardsphere maintains Stored Value balance for each user individually, independent of other users.

You are free to determine the Stored Value Balance that suits your trading needs. No minimum Stored Value Balance is required by Cardsphere to be able to participate in the marketplace.

## Acceptable Uses of Stored Value

Stored Value can be used to request items from other Cardsphere users.

Stored Value can be used to pay Cardsphere for services that Cardsphere may provide, such as membership or premium features.

Stored Value cannot be used outside of Cardsphere.

Stored Value cannot be used for the sole purpose of transferring value between users. Stored Value is only transferrable between any two users via Trustee Service provided by Cardsphere in conjunction with the marketplace activity between those two users, or by administrators to resolve disputed transactions.

## Trading

Any two registered users in good standing can engage in trading activity with the help of Cardsphere marketplace.

Cardsphere allows users to trade collectible cards, also known as Items. Each item has several properties that jointly describe the Item:

1. Name
2. Printing or Edition
3. Finish
4. Language
5. Condition

A Trade is a marketplace transaction that involves one tradable Item and two users: a Sender and a Receiver: the Sender is in possession of the Item; the Receiver has previously requested to receive such an Item. An Item is the material good with or without attached non-material value, such as promotional codes or contest entries. Non-material value may not be traded separately from the material good.

As a Cardsphere user you can assume the role of a Sender, Receiver or both.

A trade can be initiated by Senders and by Receivers:

1. by Sender by committing to send an Item to a Receiver
2. by Receiver by requesting an item from a Sender

A trade concludes when either of the following happens:

1. The Item is received by the Receiver and is verified to be as described.
2. The transaction is concluded with the help of Dispute Resolution service.

As part of a Trade, an amount of Stored Value can be transferred from Receiver to Sender via Trustee Service, as a payment for the Item.

## Overview of Sending Process

As a Sender you are expected to follow the procedure described below to send Items:

1. Using provided Collection Management Tools, you can list any number of tradable Items that you intend to make available for sending. The list of such items is called the Haves List.
2. Using provided Matchmaking Service, you can periodically check for availability of potential Trades.
3. You can decide to act upon any potential Trade and commit to send the Item to the Receiver indicated in the potential Trade.
4. After you have initiated a Trade, you must promptly ship the Item to the Receiver.
5. You should contact the Receiver via Messaging Service or open a Dispute in case when the Receiver does not mark an Item as received in a reasonable amount of time.
6. Using provided Matchmaking Service, you can can engage in passive sale of Items and expect Receivers to commit to receive Items from you.

## Trustee Service

When a Trade is initiated by a Sender, Cardsphere takes an amount of Stored Value equal to the value of the Item being traded from Receiver's Stored Value balance and holds it in trust. This way Cardsphere guarantees that the Sender can be paid by the Receiver when the Trade is concluded.

When a Trade is concluded in such a way that the Sender is entitled to receive all or a portion of the Stored Value held in trust for this Trade, Cardsphere will withhold Trustee Service Fee in the amount of the greater of the following:

1. 3% of the Stored Value to be transferred from trust to the Sender's Stored Value Balance (rounded up)
2. 1 cent

## Dispute Resolution

Cardsphere provides a mechanism to resolve disputes between users when they arise in the course of a Trade.

For the purposes of dispute resolution, a reasonable amount of time to wait is defined as 14 days for domestic mail, 30 days for International.

As a Sender you can open a dispute in conjunction to a particular trade in the following cases:

1. The Receiver has not indicated the receipt of the Item in a reasonable amount of time; in addition the Receiver cannot be reached for comment.
2. The Receiver has indicated the item is not as described.

As a Receiver you can open a dispute in conjunction to a particular trade in the following cases:

1. The Sender has not indicated the shipment of the item in a reasonable amount of time.
2. The Item has been indicated by the Sender as shipped, but has not arrived in a reasonable amount of time.
3. The item has arrived, but is not as described.

Cardsphere will have one of its administrators examine the available evidence, discuss the situation with Sender and Receiver, and issue one of the possible rulings:

1. Full Refund. Cardsphere will transfer the full amount of Stored Value held in trust for this trade to the Receiver. In addition to that, in case the Receiver chooses to return of one or more items to the Sender in accordance with the provisions of this agreement, Cardsphere will transfer an amount of Stored Value from the Sender to the Receiver for the purpose of reimbursing the Receiver for the costs associated with shipping the items back to Sender using the method specified by the Sender.
2. Split. Cardsphere will transfer a portion of the amount of Stored Value held in trust for this trade to the Receiver. Cardsphere will transfer the remaining portion of the amount of Stored Value held in trust for this trade to the Sender, minus applicable Trustee Service Fee.
3. Full Payment. Cardsphere will transfer the full amount of Stored Value held in trust for this trade to the Sender, minus applicable Trustee Service Fee.

## Dispute Resolution Guidelines

Cardsphere administrators can examine and use the following information as evidence in Dispute Resolution process:

1. Any claims and facts provided by users in dispute.
2. Contents of all Messages between users in dispute.
3. History of activity on Cardsphere of users in dispute.
4. Any material evidence provided by users in dispute (photographic images, scans, screenshots etc.)
5. Any accompanying circumstances of the dispute.

Where possible, Cardsphere and Cardsphere administrators will use the following guidelines to resolve disputes:

1. No show. Any disputes where one of the users does not respond to inquiries regarding the dispute in a reasonable time are ruled against the non-responsive user.
2. Not received. Any disputes that involve lost Items or Items that have not been shipped are ruled in favor of the Receiver as Full Refund, unless it has been established that the Receiver has violated their obligations.
3. Not as described. Any disputes that involve Items that are not as described are ruled in favor of the Receiver. Receivers may choose to return such items or adjust pricing to the appropriate amount for the condition received, and a Split ruling may be issued.

Cardsphere reserves the right to issue dispute rulings at the discretion of Cardsphere administrators.

## Reporting Card Issues

Users of the Service are provided with a feature to report current or expected price changes on cards. When enough users have reported such an issue in a 24 hour period, new trades are suspended. Trading resumes when an administrator chooses, or when the amount of reporting users drops below the threshold required in a 24 hour period to suspend trading.

Use of this feature is appropriate only when:

- A "banned and restricted" announcement involving the card has been made.
- A reprint has been announced.
- For either the above, or any other reason, a significant spike or drop in the card value has happened or is about to happen.

Reporting an issue on a card is not appropriate when the Cardsphere index price does not exactly match the price on an external source, but no rapid or significant price change has occurred or is expected to occur.
