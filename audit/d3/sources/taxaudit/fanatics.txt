## Buy Now fees
December 8 2025 7:29pm

The Fanatics Collect Buy Now Marketplace features thousands of collectibles, including sports cards, TCG, pop culture cards, comic books, memorabilia, and more. All items are available at fixed prices.

## List items for sale

Sellers can list collectibles directly from their Fanatics Collect vault at any time

- Set a fixed price for your item
- Choose whether to allow offers
- When an offer is made, respond by accepting, declining, or countering
- The minimum listing price in the Buy Now Marketplace is $0.50

## Buy collectibles instantly

Buyers can purchase fixed-price listings at any time by either paying the listed price or submitting an offer, when the seller has enabled that option.

## Buyer fees

There are no fees for purchasing in the Buy Now Marketplace — the price you see is the price you pay.

**Note:** A fulfillment fee applies if you choose to have your item shipped. Keeping it in your Fanatics Collect vault is free.

## Seller fees

The Buy Now Marketplace uses a simplified seller fee model:

- Listings priced at less than 120% of market value (as provided by Card Ladder) are charged a 6% seller fee
- Listings priced at or above 120% of market value incur a 12% seller fee

There is no minimum fee, and 6% is one of the lowest rates in the industry.

## Fee policy for offers

If your listing is priced above the Great Price threshold but you accept an offer within that threshold, the lower 6% seller fee will apply. This fee is based on the final sale price, not the original listing price.

## Skip the buyer’s premium

There are no buyer’s premiums or bonus commissions on purchases or sales.

## Expect instant payment from buyers

All Buy Now purchases are paid immediately upon checkout.
