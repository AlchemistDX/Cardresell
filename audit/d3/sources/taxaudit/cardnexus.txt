## Buyer Fees

Every time you check out, a service fee is added on top of your cart total:

|**Currency**|**Fee**|
|--|--|
|**EUR (€)**|2.5% + €0.30|
|**USD ($)**|2.5% + $0.30|
|**GBP (£)**|2.5% + £0.25|
|**CHF**|2.5% + CHF 0.30|
|**SEK**|2.5% + 3.50 SEK|
|**DKK**|2.5% + 2.25 DKK|
|**NOK**|2.5% + 3.50 NOK|
|**PLN**|2.5% + 1.30 PLN|
|**HUF**|2.5% + 120 HUF|

The fee is calculated on your entire cart (items + shipping) and applied **once per checkout**, regardless of how many sellers are in your cart. The fixed portion varies by currency to reflect approximately the same value (~€0.30 equivalent).

**Example:** You buy cards from two EU sellers for a total of €88 (including shipping). Your buyer fee is (€88 × 2.5%) + €0.30 = **€2.50**. You pay **€90.50** at checkout.

If you pay with your **seller balance**, a different fee applies: a flat **1%**, with no fixed fee.

The buyer fee is always shown clearly before you confirm your payment — no surprises.

## Seller Fees

Sellers pay a commission on each order they fulfill:

|**Region**|**Commission**|
|--|--|
|**Europe**|5% of order total|
|**North America**|8% of order total|

The "order total" includes the item subtotal and the shipping cost. The commission is deducted automatically from your payout — you never have to pay it upfront.

**Example:** A buyer purchases €55 worth of cards from you with €5 shipping (€60 total). Your seller fee is €60 × 5% = **€3.00**. You receive **€57.00**.

If a buyer uses a promo code, the discount is absorbed by CardNexus — not by you. You always receive the full sale amount minus your commission.

## Why Are NA Fees Higher Than EU?

North American transactions have higher payment processing costs and tax compliance requirements. The 8% rate reflects these additional operational costs. We're actively working to bring this down as volume grows.

## What About Coupons and Promo Codes?

When a buyer applies a promo code at checkout, **CardNexus covers the discount**. Sellers are never affected — you receive the same payout as if no coupon was used.

## Payment Methods

All payments are processed securely through **Stripe**. Depending on your region, available payment methods include:

- Credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex)
- Apple Pay and Google Pay
- Regional methods like SEPA, iDEAL, and Bancontact (EU)

CardNexus supports 9 currencies across Europe and North America. Each cart is limited to a single region (EU or North America) — you can't mix regions in one cart, but you can mix currencies within a region.

## Accepted offers

When a buyer's offer is accepted (see Receiving and answering offers), the seller commission and the buyer fee are both calculated on the agreed, discounted price — not the original listing price. Shipping is unchanged.
