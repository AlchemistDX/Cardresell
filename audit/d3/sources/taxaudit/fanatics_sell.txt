## Selling on Fanatics Collect
September 2 2026 9:36pm • Est. Read Time: 9 MIN

There are currently three ways to sell items on Fanatics Collect:

1. Weekly Auction: You can sell collectibles in the Fanatics Collect Weekly Auction by mailing them to us using our submission form. You can also sell collectibles stored in your vault through our website or mobile app. Flash allows you to submit listings from your vault to live auctions. You must submit them by 11:59 p.m. PT Wednesday for inclusion in a Weekly Auction closing that coming Sunday. Collectibles must already be in your vault to be eligible for Flash listing.
2. Buy Now: You can list vaulted collectibles in our Buy Now marketplace. You can decide if you want to allow offers from prospective buyers or list at a fixed price without allowing offers.
3. Premier Auction: Collectibles sold through the Premier Auction must have an estimated market value of at least $25,000. Any collectible submitted with a lower estimated value will require staff approval. You can submit vaulted collectibles to the Premier Auction or you can ship your collectibles to us. If you choose the shipping option, please contact our support team for shipping details.

## What is the biggest advantage of selling with Fanatics Collect?

Fanatics Collect connects buyers and sellers of trading cards and memorabilia around the world through fixed-price listings and auctions. Our comprehensive range of services, expert customer service, and auctions and Buy Now marketplace that host tens of thousands of listings spanning common collection builders to some of the most sought-after cards in the industry make us the number one source for thousands of collectors around the world. Selling on Fanatics Collect can lead to higher sale prices compared to other venues. And our fees, including our simplified Buy Now marketplace seller’s fees in which users can earn up to 94% on any sale under $120, are lower than many other auction houses. Finally, Fanatics Collect handles photographing, titling, listing, receiving and distributing payment, and ownership transfer for all sales so you don’t have to worry about who’s on the other end of your sale.

## What types of items can I list for sale?

We currently accept:

- Authenticated trading cards and small slabbed collectibles from PSA, SGC, BGS, BVG, BCCG, CGC CARDS, CSG, MBA.
- Non-authenticated (raw) trading cards when using one of our authentication partners
- Authenticated video games from WATA, CGC
- Authenticated oversized slabbed Items from PSA, BGS, CGC, CBCS, WATA, VGA
- Authenticated vintage sports wax from BBCE, GAI, PSA, and factory-sealed modern sports wax
- Authenticated TCG wax
- Baseballs, hockey pucks, or other small-sized memorabilia authenticated by UDA, Steiner, Fanatics Authentic, or any other reputable third-party authenticator
- Authenticated memorabilia from MeiGray, Resolution, Davious, RGU, or any other reputable third-party authenticator
- Authenticated wax cases from BBCE and factory-sealed wax cases

We DO NOT accept:

- Non-authenticated vintage wax boxes or packs, memorabilia, comics, or video games
- Non-authenticated TCG wax
- Coins and currency

Our team of experts meticulously reviews each card for authenticity, checking for any signs of holder tampering or attempting to submit fakes or reprints. This thorough process ensures every item sold on our site meets the highest standards of quality and provides the best experience for our customers.

## Why don’t I see my Weekly Auction submission in my Member Dashboard?

Within two days of receiving your submission, we mark it as received on your account and send you an email notification. When we begin processing your submission, including imaging, titling, and prepping for sale, we mark your items as processing. You can see this status in your Member Dashboard and on the mobile app. Images, titles, and descriptions will be visible at least two days before the start of the Weekly Auction. We will notify you when your listings are available for review.

## How do I fix an error in my listings?

We will give you an opportunity to request edits through your Member Dashboard or the mobile app before the auction begins. Our Asset Control team will approve the request or provide you with feedback.

All Weekly Auction listings start with an opening bid of $5. Premier Auction items begin with an opening bid of $1,000.

## What happens if my Weekly Auction listings go unpaid or unsold?

If a winning bidder does not pay for a listing it will be relisted in the next equivalent auction.

If your auction listing does not sell, your collectible will be sent back to your Fanatics Collect Vault. From there your collectible is eligible for relisting in one upcoming Fanatics Collect auction. If it does not sell in that second auction, it will be returned to your Vault once again, where you must wait 90 days before relisting it again. Please note that if your collectible is not listed in an auction on the 1st of any given month, you will be charged the standard one-time-only $3 Fanatics Collect Vault storage fee.

## What are the steps to creating a submission?

You can sell collectibles by mailing them to us using our submission form. Below are the simple steps you need to follow to complete your submission:

1. **Create submission**
   **I. Web:** Choose from the collectible categories, such as trading cards, raw trading cards, memorabilia, etc.
   **II. Mobile:** Simply select "Sell" on the top right menu, then select "Non-Vaulted Items"
2. **Indicate the authentication status:** Specify whether your card is authenticated or unauthenticated (RAW). We do no accept non-authenticated Vintage Sports Wax Boxes or Packs, TCG Wax, Memorabilia, Comics, or Video Games.
3. **Enter the number of items**: List the total number of items you are submitting.
4. **Provide the estimated value**: Include your best estimate for the value of your items.
5. **Indicate selling intent: ** List immediately to auction, or simply send to your vault.

If you are submitting a **RAW unauthenticated card**, you must select one of the available authentication options. After completing the steps above, you will be able to **download your packing slip**, which will include all necessary details to send the items to our vault. Once we receive your items, they will be securely vaulted, authenticated (if applicable), and available to consign for auction or Buy Now.

## What are the deadlines for submission?

Our Premier Auction submission deadlines are posted on our homepage. We accept Weekly Auction submissions on a rolling basis. Weekly Flash listings must be submitted by 11:59 p.m. PT on Wednesday to be included in that week’s Sunday closing. Premier Auction Flash listings must be submitted by 11:59 p.m. PT on the Saturday before the auction’s Thursday night closing.

## Can I bid on my items to ensure a reserve price or to watch my items?

Clients bidding on their items is strictly prohibited and a violation of our Marketplace Tenets. Our marketplace software is designed to restrict bidding by a client on an item they submit.

## I see my submission has been delivered. Do you have it?

Although a package will show as being delivered to our facility, our team has to process the submission and record it in our database. There may be a delay of a few days between package delivery and the package showing up in your Vault, especially if it is eligible for an Eye Appeal review. You will receive an email confirmation when the package is successfully processed.

## Does Fanatics Collect pay for my shipping when I send a submission?

You are responsible for the cost of sending your submission, but Fanatics Collect can assist with the shipping process by generating a fully insured label. Please contact our support team for more information.

## Can I deliver my items to the Fanatics Collect Vault in person?

Unfortunately, we do not have an in-person drop-off option for security reasons. All packages must arrive through the mail. If you have high-value items you do not know how to ship and insure, we can provide you with a low-cost, fully insured overnight FedEx shipping label. However, we do accept submissions at most trade shows we attend.

## How should I pack my collectibles for shipping to Fanatics Collect?

You can send your package using your preferred shipping carrier. However, we recommend you mail your collectibles in a box with proper protective padding tracking information. For more valuable collectibles, it may be advantageous to double-box them to ensure safe delivery. We also ask that you send such packages with signature confirmation.

For high-value submissions that are difficult to insure through standard carriers, Fanatics Collect can assist with a fully insured shipping label. Please contact our support team to request a label.

## What are the Premier Auction seller's fees?

There is no seller’s fee for the Premier Auction, but there is a buyer’s premium. Sellers also receive a bonus commission based on the highest bid the listing attracts. A minimum of $1,000 of the price realized is paid to Fanatics Collect on each Premier Auction listing.

- The hammer price refers to the maximum winning bid, excluding the 20% buyer's premium. If an item sells for $250,000 or lower, the seller receives 110% of the hammer price.
- If an item sells for $250,001 - $1,000,000, the seller receives 112.5% of the hammer price.
- If an item sells for $1,000,001 or higher, the seller receives 115% of the hammer price.

## Is the auction fee calculated on the total value of my submission?

Auction fees are applied based on the sale price of each individual listing, not the sum total of the submission overall.

## When will I receive payment for sold items?
- *Weekly Sunday/Weekly Sunday Flash:* Buyers have five (5) days to remit payment after receiving an invoice. There is a grace period provided to allow for ACH and wire payments to clear. Sellers are paid out within three (3) business days of completed payment processing.
- *Buy Now:* Payment is required at checkout. However, if an offer is accepted, buyers have five (5) days to remit payment after receiving an invoice. There is a grace period provided to allow for ACH and wire payments to clear. Sellers are paid out within three (3) business days of completed payment processing.
- *Premier Auction:* Buyers have ten (10) days to remit payment after receiving an invoice. There is a grace period provided to allow for ACH and wire payments to clear. Sellers are paid out within three (3) business days of completed payment processing.

## What are Flash listings?

Flash-listed items join the currently active auction and end when that auction ends.

You must submit collectibles before 11:59 p.m. PT on Wednesday to be included in the Weekly Auction closing that Sunday. Otherwise, your item will close in the following Weekly Auction.

You must submit collectibles before 11:59 p.m. PT on Sunday to be included in the Premier Auction closing the following Thursday. Otherwise, your item will be listed in the next Premier Auction.

## What are the benefits of Flashing into a Weekly Auction?

Flashing into a Weekly Auction gives you more control over the selling process. If you want to try to capitalize on an event (e.g., an NBA player winning a title), you can Flash a collectible for sale immediately.

## How do I Flash my items into the Weekly Auction?

From your Fanatics Collect Vault, select the items you want to sell and choose the “LIST NOW” option. The next screen will ask you to select the marketplace for your item (e.g., Weekly Sunday, Weekly Sunday Flash, Monthly Premier, or Buy Now). Select the “Weekly Sunday Flash” option and follow the directions.

## Can I mail items to Fanatics Collect to Flash into a Weekly Auction?

You can only Flash items from your Vault into an auction. You can mail items to your Vault and list them in a later auction after we curate them.

## How long does it take for my items to be Flashed into auction?

Items appear in the auction within 24 hours, but will likely appear much sooner.

## What is the Weekly Flash deadline?

You must Flash items by Wednesday at 11:59 p.m. PT for them to be included in that week’s Sunday closing. They will appear in the Weekly Sunday Auction within 24 hours, usually much sooner.

## When will I get paid for items sold via Flash?

The payment process for Flashed listings is the same as other Weekly Sunday Auction and Premier Auction listings. After an auction closes, buyers have five (5) days to remit payment for a listing won in a Weekly Auction and ten (10) days to remit payment for a listing won in a Premier Auction. Fanatics Collect pays sellers within one (1) business day of completed payment processing. Fanatics Collect issues payments to sellers' Fanatics Collect accounts, from which sellers can use funds to pay Fanatics Collect invoices or initiate a payout request to have funds issued to them.
