The fees on the site are:



**Marketplace Fee**

- The Mana Pool marketplace fee is 5% of the price of the merchandise

- the fee is not applied to shipping charges, only the price of the product



**Credit Card Fees**

- We charge Stripe's credit card fees (2.9% + 30 cents as of 12-7-23).

- The only markup on this is that we charge $0.30 per seller, per order, while we are only charged per order. This avoids complication in our software code, and it provides a buffer for chargebacks and refunds.
