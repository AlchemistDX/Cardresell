The following are the different types of Commission Fees for utilizing COMC:

**Transaction Fee:** Fixed-price sales, whether sold on the COMC platform or via eBay have a flat 5% transaction fee of the item's sale price. The transaction fee is charged when the card is sold. Auction sale transaction fees are slightly lower. Please review our Auction page here for more information.

**Cash-out Fee:** To withdraw proceeds from sales, or withdraw credit deposited into your COMC account, there is a 10% Cash-Out fee. This fee percentage may be slightly higher for international users. Additionally, if the funds are request as a check rather than PayPal deposit, there is a small check fee. Learn more about the Cash-Out process from our dedicated FAQ section here.
