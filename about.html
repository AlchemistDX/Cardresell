From card to selling plan

  1. Find and review your card

    Search by name or use photo identification. Check the set, number, condition, and finish. Bulk scans estimate prices as results arrive; the optional Finish / printing control lets you correct a match.

  2. Compare your options

    Estimate proceeds after fees and shipping, consider selling low-value cards together, or compare grading costs and published turnaround estimates before submitting.

  3. Save your work and prepare to sell

    Keep cards in Collection, prepare supported listing drafts for review and export, and record completed sales. A draft or export does not publish a marketplace listing.

A stack of $1 cards needs a different plan

Individual shipping and selling fees can consume the value of low-priced cards. Use the bulk and lot guidance to compare selling together, local-shop offers, or buylist quotes. Retail value is not a buyer offer; the buyer must confirm acceptance, condition, and the final payout.

What the estimates mean

Lookup supports Pokémon, Magic, Yu-Gi-Oh!, Lorcana, and One Piece, with coverage varying by set and printing. Available market prices are guides; automatic eBay sold data may be unavailable. Follow the marketplace links to research comparable sales, and review the sources, dates, and limitations.

Photo-based grade estimates assess visible centering, corners, edges, and surface. They are not professional grades, authentication, or a guarantee of a grading company's result.

How we make money

Verified Free accounts receive 10 ID + 1 Grade credit once, plus 5 ID + 1 Grade credit each month. Issued credits never expire. Monthly paid plans are Starter $4.99, Casual $9.99, Pro $19.99, and Business $49.99. Upgrading adds scanning and grading allowances, supported venue comparisons, seller tools, and eligible credit-pack savings. See the plan comparison for current benefits and pack prices. When you click through to an eBay or TCGplayer listing we may earn an affiliate credit; it does not change the price you pay or influence our ranking.

What we’re not

CardResell is not a grading company, not a marketplace, and not affiliated with The Pokémon Company, Nintendo, PSA, BGS, CGC, or SGC. Prices and fees are estimates — always verify with the actual marketplace before you list.
