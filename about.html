About CardResell

Independent · Built by resellers, for resellers

CardResell is a lightweight tool that helps trading-card sellers answer one question fast: where do I actually make the most money on this card, after fees?

Every marketplace advertises "low fees." In practice, eBay, TCGplayer, Mercari, Poshmark, Whatnot, Fanatics Collect, COMC, and a dozen buylists each take a different cut, add different shipping quirks, and pay out at different speeds. We pull the real numbers together across 15 venues so you can list where the payout is highest — not where the marketing is loudest.

What we do

  • Card Lookup — Search any Pokémon, Magic, Yu-Gi-Oh!, Lorcana, or One Piece card and see live sold comps (English + Japanese Pokémon).
  • Fee breakdown — Same card, every marketplace, side by side. Actual net payout — not "list price."
  • Estimated Grade Scan — Photo a raw card and get an in-app centering / corners / edges / surface estimate before you drop $30+ on PSA/BGS.
  • Sales Tracker — Log what you bought, what you sold it for, and where. Watch your realized P&L instead of guessing.

How we make money

Free tier gets you 10 scans + 1 AI Grade the day you verify your email, then 5 scans + 1 Grade every month after that — credits never expire. Paid tiers unlock more monthly credits, more venues, and the full fee breakdown: Pro $9.99/mo and Pro Max $19.99/mo, plus annual plans at a discount. One-time credit packs are pay-as-you-go from ~$0.20 per ID and ~$0.60 per Grade. When you click through to an eBay or TCGplayer listing we may earn an affiliate credit — it never changes the price you pay, and it never influences ranking.

What we’re not

CardResell is not a grading company, not a marketplace, and not affiliated with The Pokémon Company, Nintendo, PSA, BGS, CGC, or SGC. Prices and fees are estimates — always verify with the actual marketplace before you list.
