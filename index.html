How do you want it graded?
Grade estimates are only as good as the photos. Deep Grade sees corners and edges — the two things that most often bust a Quick estimate.
RECOMMENDED
🔍 Deep Grade
4–6 photos · front, back + 2–4 edges
Measures centering · inspects each corner · sees edge dings Quick misses · CV-verified pillars
Use before submitting to PSA / BGS / CGC
2 credits
⚡ Quick Grade
2 photos · front + back only
Fast look for raw cards you already know are clean — skips corner + edge inspection
1 credit
📸
How to take a great grading photo
Follow these and your grade estimate will match a real grader within 1 point far more often. The AI only sees what your camera shows it.
💡
Bright, even, indirect light
Near a window during the day is ideal. Kill glare hotspots and finger shadows.
📤
Remove sleeves, toploaders, and slabs
Plastic reflects light and hides flaws. Take the bare card out — you can put it back after.
🎯
Fill the frame, aligned straight
Card edges close to the photo edges, held parallel — not tilted.
🧼
Plain, uniform background
Solid dark surface. No clutter, no wood grain, no fabric.
🔍
Tack-sharp focus, no motion blur
Tap to focus, hold steady, count "one" before releasing. Blurry photos hide the flaws that decide the grade.

CardResell
Built by a card flipper, for card flippers
CardResell was built by Will Jones in Bradenton, Florida to solve a problem every reseller hits — you know your card is worth money but you have no idea which platform actually pays you the most after fees.

The app pulls live sold comps from eBay and real-time prices from the TCG market, then calculates your exact take-home on every major platform side by side — eBay, COMC, Poshmark, Fanatics Collect, and PWCC — so you never leave money on the table.

The AI Grading Estimate uses GPT-4o vision to analyze centering, corners, edges, and surface — giving you a realistic grade prediction before you spend money on a PSA submission.
