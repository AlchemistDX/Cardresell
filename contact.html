Refunds

Failed-scan credit refunds: eligible identification failures receive an automatic refund or offer “Not my card — refund credit” on the result. If the app cannot confirm a refund, send the displayed support reference to will@cardresell.org. Include the approximate time and what happened. Never send your password or payment-card details.

Subscriptions can be cancelled anytime from inside the app and continue through the end of the paid period. We do not prorate or refund partial periods.

Credit packs are non-refundable once purchased. Credits never expire, so anything you buy stays on your account. Full policy in our Terms.
